# Revised experiment: adaptive accuracy, efficiency and failure resilience

The intended claim is that quality-aware adaptive multimodal processing can preserve stress-detection accuracy while reducing computation and retaining useful operation when ECG fails. Version 1 missed the combined compute/recall target. This revision changes the method before rerunning evaluation; the target remains 25% lower CPU cost with at most three percentage points of all-window stress-recall loss against fusion, and at least 80% clean coverage.

Changes fixed before the revised results:

1. Separate ECG early-exit confidence from final expert acceptance. Search class-specific exit thresholds in {0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9, 0.95, 0.99, 1.0} and final acceptance in {0.5, 0.6, 0.7}, requiring exit confidence at least final confidence.
2. Select the least-escalating policy satisfying coverage, pooled stress recall, participant-macro stress recall and operational-error constraints on policy participants. Reference full-evidence fusion, falling back to the alternate expert when ECG fails and ECG when fusion is unavailable. Abstention counts as an operational error. Enforce constraints on clean and combined development scenarios. Report infeasibility explicitly and use full-evidence fallback.
3. Expand the existing inner grouped model search with two SVM configurations (C=10, gamma=scale or 0.01) and a compact forest (64 trees, depth 6, minimum leaf size 3). Weight participants equally during selection. Keep fit, calibration and policy participants disjoint. Historical baselines retain their original estimator settings.
4. For newly trained bundles, reject ECG whose lag-one roughness, mean(diff(x)^2)/(2*var(x)), exceeds 0.5. White noise approaches one; this fixed, scale-invariant engineering heuristic targets high-frequency corruption. It is not a validated clinical quality index and is specific to the current 700 Hz chest ECG. Preserve prior bundle behavior by leaving the new gate disabled when absent from their configuration. Quality failure routes to EDA/respiration, not to a manufactured baseline.
5. Preserve the original datasets, artifacts and reports. Produce versioned v2 outputs, waveform-based LOSO evaluation and a separate host benchmark. Do not select a new rule from the revised outer results.

The first development fold exposed an infeasible coverage constraint when EDA dropout prevented fusion after a usable ECG requested more evidence. Before inspecting outer predictions, the initial run was interrupted and retained at reports/multimodal-real-v2-initial-attempt. The completed revision lets usable ECG pass its final acceptance rule when fusion is unavailable, reports that reason explicitly, and applies the identical fallback in policy selection. Failed ECG still requires usable alternate channels; total dropout still abstains. This correction uses development diagnostics, not held-out outcomes.

The same WESAD participants have already been analyzed. Nested participant separation prevents direct fitting/tuning leakage within this run, but this is a revised, exploratory analysis of an observed dataset, not an independent confirmatory test. Further revisions based on these results require another version and ultimately a new cohort. Physical edge-device and sensor-energy claims remain outside the available evidence.

Calibration continues to use independent participants, consistent with [scikit-learn's calibration guidance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/calibration.html). The grouped search uses explicit [SVC C and gamma settings](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.svm.SVC.html).
