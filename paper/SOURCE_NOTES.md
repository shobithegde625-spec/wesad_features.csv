## Experimental evidence

The manuscript's numbers come from `reports/multimodal-real-v2/comparison.csv`, `summary.json`, `paired_differences.json`, `predictions.csv` and `targets.json`; the version comparison uses the retained v1 outputs. Final-artifact timings come separately from `reports/multimodal-real-benchmark-v2/benchmark.json` and `timings.csv`. The artifact identity is `dae3a7c563c9`.

The dataframe tables and number macros are regenerated by `tools/build_assets.py`. Diagrams are newly written TikZ. The confusion matrices are newly plotted saved predictions. The waveform diagnostic was generated by the project's feature inspection. Dashboard screenshots were captured from the actual local Streamlit application; presentation-only CSS and capture details are documented in `figures/screenshots.json`.

The 434 eligible replay windows differ from the 445 training windows because the two datasets use different documented window alignment/inclusion rules. Mixed/unsupported replay conditions remain unscored. No new training, cherry-picked test thresholds, physical-device energy measurements, similarity-report score or clinical study was added for this manuscript.
